Why this differs from Meta's reported numbers

Each ad platform claims credit for the same sale, so summing their dashboards overcounts revenue. A blended model attributes each order once across the real customer journey. Last click rewards the final touch (often email/branded search); first click rewards discovery (often Meta/TikTok); linear and time decay spread credit. The gap between these is exactly the visibility a pixel-based tool buys you.
